# Game ideas

## Done
- [x] **Train Yard** — tap trains to drive them out of the yard; blocked trains go red and beep. No lives, play until clear.

## To do
| Game | Why it suits him |
|---|---|
| **Memory Match** | Flip cards to find pairs of animals or vehicles. Easy to change the difficulty (6 to 16 cards). |
| **Bubble/Balloon Pop** | Pop floating balloons. Each one says a number or colour, so he learns while playing. |
| **Connect the Colours** | Draw a line joining matching coloured dots without crossing (like "Flow"). A similar puzzle style to the trains. |
| **Simple Jigsaw** | 4, 6 or 9 big pieces made from a cartoon picture that snap into place. |
| **Paint & Colour** | Tap to fill in outline pictures, or free drawing with rainbow brushes. |
| **Animal Piano / Xylophone** | Tap keys for notes or animal sounds. Pure fun with no goal. |
| **Mazes** | Drag a little car through a maze to the garage. |
| **Shape Sorter** | Drag shapes into matching holes. |
| **Dot-to-Dot** | Tap numbers in order to reveal a picture, which helps with counting. |

Quickest to build: Memory Match, Balloon Pop. Closest in feel to Train Yard: Connect the Colours.

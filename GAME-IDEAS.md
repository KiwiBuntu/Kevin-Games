# Game ideas

## Done
- [x] **Train Yard** — tap trains to drive them out of the yard; blocked trains go red and beep. No lives, play until clear.
- [x] **Memory Match** — flip cards to find pairs; 3 / 6 / 10 pairs; animals, vehicles, food, sea creatures; names spoken on a match.
- [x] **Balloon Pop** — tap rising balloons; colours / numbers / letters / animals spoken aloud; rainbow star balloons; 20 pops to win. ✏️ Spelling mode: pop the letters of a spoken word (KEVIN, GRANNY, GRANDPA, MUM, DAD, sight words…); 3 words to win. Grown-ups edit the word list behind 🔒 (sum lock) in spelling mode; the original list is at the top of `www/games/balloons/balloons.js`.
- [x] **Connect the Colours** — drag lines between matching smiley dots without crossing; 5×5 / 6×6 / 8×8; hint shows one route; no need to fill the board.
- [x] **Jigsaw** — 4 / 6 / 9 real jigsaw-shaped pieces; six cartoon scenes drawn in code; faint picture guide; generous snapping.
- [x] **Shape Sorter** — drag smiley shapes into matching holes in a wooden box; 3 / 5 / 8 shapes; names spoken.
- [x] **Dot to Dot** — tap or slide through numbered (or lettered) dots; six pictures that colour in when finished.
- [x] **Mazes** — drag the car / mouse / puppy / rocket / bee through a hedge maze to its goal; three sizes; hint shows the way.
- [x] **Animal Piano** — rainbow xylophone; mode button cycles 🎵 notes / 🐮 real animal recordings (CC0, see `www/games/piano/sounds/CREDITS.md`) / 🗣️ silly voices; follow-the-glowing-bar songs (Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Row Your Boat).
- [x] **Paint** — tap-to-fill colouring pictures (7) or free drawing with a rainbow brush; cheer when a picture is fully coloured. 💾 saves to a 🖼️ gallery on the device (tap to keep painting, 📤 share/save to phone, 🗑️ twice to delete).
- [x] **Parking Garage** — slide cars back and forth so each drives out of the door matching its colour; every car that leaves makes room; tap to drive toward the door, drag to slide; hint, undo; puzzles checked by a solver.
- [x] **Read Along** — bookshelf of stories read aloud with the words lighting up; 🐢🚶🐇 speeds; tap a word to hear it; remembers his page; voice + speed saved per book. Grown-ups (behind a sum, answer 100–200) paste, type or load .txt/.md stories with light Markdown: `# Heading`, `**strong**`, `*soft*`, `---` new page. Books stay on the tablet. **Family readings:** grown-ups record themselves (any name + face) reading a book page by page; Kevin picks who reads (👴 / 👵 / 🗣️) and the words light up in time with the recording; 📤 export / 📥 import a reading as one file to move it between devices; pages whose words change are flagged to record again.
- [x] **Number Tower** — climb a tower by shooting a web to the right answer window (Super Kevin, Gecko Kid or Robo); no falling, wrong windows just go "boing"; milestones every 10 floors as the sky changes to clouds, sunset, night and space. Activities: Counting (keeps going), How many?, ➕ ➖ ✖️ ➗ and Mixed. Grown-ups (sum lock) choose which activities show, the biggest answer (10/20/50/100), 2 or 3 choices and picture help (dots).
- [x] **Cube Pop** — (made harder 2026-10-10: every block split 2×2×2, inside painted in "onion" layers of new colours, rainbow every 8 shots) 3D block models (cube → dinosaur, 12 levels unlocking in order); colour blasters pop every block of that colour you can see; drag to spin (snaps to 4 views); hidden colours spin into view; 🌈 rainbow bonus; blasters show how many blocks they'll pop. Models are easy to add in `www/games/cubes/levels.js`.
- [x] **Potion Sort** — tap a flask then another to pour; sort every colour into its own flask (4 per flask). Starts at 2 colours + 1 empty, one more colour every 3 levels up to 7, endless after that; every level is checked by a solver. 🪄 = unlimited undo (the only help — no hints); when the puzzle can't be finished any more the wand glows and a once-per-level +🧪 spare flask is offered (grown-ups can switch the spare off behind 🔒).
- [x] **Candy Pop** — match-3 with 30 levels on a map: goals (collect colours, clear jelly, break crates, bring cherries down), ice, holes and odd board shapes. Specials: striped (match 4), wrapped bomb (L/T), rainbow (match 5), plus combos when two specials are swapped. Gentle failure: move limits set from simulated play; "Out of moves!" offers 🎁 +5 once, or 🔄 instant retry. 1–3 stars; hint wiggle after 8s.
